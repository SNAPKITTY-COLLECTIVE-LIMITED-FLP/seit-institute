## ARTICLE II — MISSION STATEMENT

**The Mission of SEIT is:**

To educate, certify, and advance the science of Sovereign Artificial Intelligence —
autonomous computing systems that govern themselves through immutable structural axioms,
operate on hardware they control, and produce cryptographically verifiable audit trails
accessible to the public.

**SEIT pursues its mission through three programs:**

1. **The Sovereign AI Standard** — A published, peer-reviewed framework defining what
   constitutes a genuinely sovereign AI system. Freely available to the public.

2. **The Certification Program** — Independent third-party auditing of AI systems
   against the Sovereign AI Standard, resulting in issued Observer, Sovereign, or
   Igneous certification marks.

3. **The Institute** — Educational programs, research publications, workshops, and
   open curriculum teaching sovereign AI architecture principles.

## ARTICLE III — NGO PURPOSE & INTERNATIONAL STANDING

### 3.1 Organizational Purpose
SEIT is organized as a non-governmental organization (NGO) for the international
advancement of sovereign AI governance standards. SEIT operates in the public interest
as a standards body, certification authority, and research institution.

**Primary activities:**
- **(a) Standards Development:** Authoring and publishing the Sovereign AI Standard —
  a publicly available technical and governance framework defining verifiable AI sovereignty.
- **(b) Certification:** Operating an independent certification program issuing
  Observer, Sovereign, and Igneous marks to qualifying AI systems globally.
- **(c) Research & Publication:** Advancing the science of autonomous compute systems,
  emergent multi-agent behavior, and cryptographic governance verification.
- **(d) Education:** Open curriculum, workshops, and publications on sovereign AI architecture.

### 3.2 UN-Associated NGO Status
SEIT is established with the intent to pursue **UN ECOSOC Consultative Status** through
the United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs (UN DESA).

ECOSOC consultative status positions SEIT as a recognized international body able to:
- Participate in UN conferences and meetings on AI governance
- Submit written and oral statements to ECOSOC bodies
- Contribute to international AI policy frameworks
- Collaborate with UN agencies (UNESCO, ITU, UNDP) on sovereign AI standards

**ECOSOC application pathway:**
1. Operate as a registered NGO for minimum 2 years (clock starts 2026-05-21)
2. Demonstrate international scope and membership
3. Submit application to UN DESA Committee on NGOs
4. Application window: 2028 (earliest eligible, given 2-year operational requirement)

### 3.3 IRC Tax Classification

## ARTICLE IV — RELATIONSHIP WITH SNAPKITTY COLLECTIVE

SEIT operates **in care of** the SnapKitty Collective under the following terms:

### 4.1 Fiscal Sponsorship
The SnapKitty Collective serves as fiscal sponsor for SEIT during the period prior to
SEIT obtaining its own 501(c)(3) determination letter from the IRS. Under this
arrangement:

- Donations designated for SEIT programs are received and held by SnapKitty Collective
- SnapKitty Collective grants funds to SEIT programs upon request from the Founding Director
- SnapKitty Collective's tax-exempt status covers SEIT activities during the sponsorship period
- This arrangement terminates automatically upon SEIT receiving its own 501(c)(3) determination

### 4.2 Operational Independence
SEIT maintains independent governance, standards, and certification authority.
SnapKitty Collective does not control SEIT's technical standards or certification decisions.

### 4.3 Reference Implementation
The SnapKitty OS / SACM mesh operated by SnapKitty Collective serves as the living
reference implementation of the Sovereign AI Standard. SEIT holds Igneous Certification
over this system. This relationship is symbiotic — not a conflict of interest —
because SEIT's certification standards are published, public, and independently auditable.

### 4.4 IP Distinction
- **SnapKitty Collective IP** (commercial): SnapKitty OS, SACM, Magma Protocol, ProxyGuild,
  AdapterAxiom — proprietary, licensed, owned by Ahmad Ali Parr
- **SEIT IP** (public benefit): Sovereign AI Standard, certification framework,
  attack corpus (Tier A), educational curriculum — freely published
- **SEIT Marks** (institutional): Saint Errant Observer Mark, Sovereign Mark, Igneous Mark
  — certification marks issued to qualifying systems by SEIT, not sold or licensed
